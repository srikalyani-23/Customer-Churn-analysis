# SaaS Customer Churn & Retention Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes SaaS customer data to identify churn patterns, understand customer behavior, and support data-driven retention strategies.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor customer churn, retention, revenue impact, customer segments, and key business KPIs.

The project demonstrates skills in data analysis, data cleaning, KPI development, business intelligence, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts recurring revenue and long-term business growth.

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention
- Identify customer segments with higher churn
- Understand factors associated with customer churn
- Measure revenue at risk due to churn
- Identify high-value customers requiring retention attention
- Provide actionable insights through an interactive dashboard

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks important SaaS business metrics including:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Retained Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Revenue at Risk
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Churn Probability
- Retention Priority

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis across multiple dimensions:

### Customer Analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Churn vs retention distribution
- Customer demographics and characteristics
- High-risk customer identification

### Churn Analysis
- Overall churn rate
- Churn patterns across customer segments
- Factors associated with customer churn
- Comparison of churned and retained customers

### Revenue Analysis
- Revenue contribution by customer segment
- Revenue at risk from churn
- Customer value analysis
- Identification of high-value customers

### Retention Analysis
- Retention performance
- High-priority customers
- Customer risk segmentation
- Retention-focused business insights
